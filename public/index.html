What it watches

Liquidation distance

Edge-triggered when mark price gets within your threshold of a position's bust price. One alert, not one per minute.

Position lifecycle

Opened, closed with net PnL and PAPER minted, or liquidated. Follow top traders for opens and closes.

Session keys and rewards

Warns before a session key lapses and when staking rewards cross the amount you care about.

Protocol signals

PAPER mint rate thresholds and markets going paused or close-only.

Telegram, Discord, webhooks

Retries with backoff, a dead-letter list, and HMAC-SHA256 signed webhooks you can verify.

Agent-ready

A stateless MCP server with ten tools, an OpenAPI document, llms.txt and agent cards. Read-only on funds: it can never move money.

Quickstart

  1. Create a subscription below. Copy the token: it is shown once.
  2. Add a Discord webhook or a webhook endpoint under Channels and press Send test.
  3. Add a wallet under Wallets. The first poll is silent, so existing positions are not announced.
